About Us

The story and approach behind TRICODE Academy.

Academy Introduction

Our story

TRICODE Academy was created to give students a modern, practical place to learn technology and creative skills.

Our name and our logo reflect three ideas that guide everything we teach: education, technology and innovation. Students come to learn skills such as web development, programming, graphic design and video editing in a friendly, focused environment.

Content note: Founding year, founder’s message and milestones will be added once supplied by the academy.

Our Mission

Draft: To give every student practical, job-ready technology and creative skills through clear, hands-on teaching.

Our Vision

Draft: To be a trusted academy where education, technology and innovation come together to prepare confident learners.

Why TRICODE

What makes us different.

  • Practical, hands-on learning in every class
  • Three clear levels: Basic, Mid and Advance
  • Flexible class shifts, Monday to Friday
  • Technology and creative courses in one academy

Teaching Approach

Practical, step by step.

  1. 1

    Learn the idea

    Short, clear explanations in each 40-minute class.

  2. 2

    Practise on a computer

    Students apply it straight away in the lab.

  3. 3

    Build and improve

    Small tasks grow into real, usable skills.

  4. 4

    Level up

    Move from Basic to Mid to Advance at your own pace.
